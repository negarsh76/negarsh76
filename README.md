## 👩‍💻 About me

I'm an **AI researcher and Machine Learning Engineer** working where computer vision meets the real world: medical images taken on smartphones, insurance claims, and noisy, imperfect labels.

- 🔬 **Research:** medical image analysis, uncertainty-aware learning, and learning with noisy and ambiguous labels
- 🩺 **M.Sc. thesis:** *Automatic Pharyngitis Type Detection from Smartphone Images under Uncertain Labels* (Persian Gulf University)
- 🚗 **Now:** ML Engineer at **Day Insurance**, building vehicle-damage assessment, claim-fraud detection, and OCR/NLP pipelines for medical and insurance documents
- 📱 **On the side:** Persian-first mobile apps with Kotlin Multiplatform and Jetpack Compose
- 🎯 **Looking for:** PhD opportunities in medical imaging and trustworthy AI

### 🕸️ [Patient-Similarity Graphs](https://github.com/negarsh76/Patient-Similarity-Graph)
Pharyngitis type classification as a **graph problem**: patients are linked by symptom similarity, then grouped with Louvain communities and node2vec-style embeddings, and classified with label propagation and graph-augmented features. Adding the graph raises macro-F1 by about 3 points when only 3–5 % of diagnoses are known. *(Synthetic data.)*

`NetworkX` `scikit-learn` `Graph ML`

### 🔍 [DeepDuplicate](https://github.com/negarsh76/DeepDuplicate)
Finds exact, near and visual duplicates in large image collections. A **CLIP / DINOv2 + FAISS** search feeds a **rotation-aware pHash → SSIM → SIFT + RANSAC** cascade. It ships with a CLI, a GUI, an HTML report and a reproducible benchmark (0.99 precision, F1 0.75 vs 0.57 for pHash alone).

`PyTorch` `OpenCLIP` `DINOv2` `FAISS` `OpenCV`
